# Department Notice DEPT-CSE-NOTICE-2026-09: Laboratory Attendance and Practical Examination

Doc ID: DEPT-CSE-NOTICE-2026-09
Version: 1.0
Issued by: Head, Department of Computer Science and Engineering
Effective from: 2026-09-01

<!-- page: 1 -->
## 1. Laboratory Attendance

1.1 In addition to the University-wide attendance requirement, a student of the Department of Computer
Science and Engineering shall have a minimum of **90 percent** attendance in the laboratory sessions of
each laboratory course of the Department.

1.2 A student who does not meet the requirement in clause 1.1 shall not be permitted to appear for the
practical examination of that laboratory course, and the laboratory internal assessment shall be
recorded as zero.

1.3 The requirement in clause 1.1 is a departmental requirement for laboratory courses of this
Department. It does not alter the requirement for the theory courses of any course.

## 2. Practical Examination

2.1 The practical examination is conducted by a board of two examiners appointed by the Head of the
Department.

2.2 A student must produce the laboratory record book, duly signed by the faculty in charge, at the
practical examination. A candidate without a signed record book is examined under a separate
procedure approved by the Head of the Department.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## 3. Project Evaluation

3.1 Project work registered in the Department is evaluated at the end of the semester by the project
guide and one examiner appointed by the Head of the Department.

3.2 A project submitted after the notified date attracts a penalty of 10 percent of the project marks.

3.3 A student whose project is assessed as unsatisfactory is given one opportunity to resubmit within
four weeks.

## 4. Departmental Communication

4.1 This notice shall be displayed on the Department notice board and circulated to all course
coordinators of the Department.
