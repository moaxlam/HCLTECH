# Student Council Notice: Attendance Relief Demand

Doc ID: STUCOUNCIL-NOTICE-2026-09
Version: 1.0
Issued by: Students' Council (unofficial, not an office of the University)
Effective from: 2026-09-20

<!-- page: 1 -->
## 1. Attendance Relief

1.1 Following representations by students, the Students' Council has decided that the minimum
attendance requirement for the current semester shall be **60 percent** for all courses, and that
students with attendance above 60 percent shall be treated as eligible for the end-semester
examination.

1.2 The Council requests all departments to apply the relief stated in clause 1.1 to every student of
the 2023 and 2024 batches.

1.3 Students may quote this notice at the time of hall ticket collection.

## 2. Status of this Notice

2.1 This notice is issued by the Students' Council on its own authority.

2.2 The Council is not the Registrar, the Dean (Academics) or the Controller of Examinations, and this
notice has not been approved, endorsed or ratified by any of them.

2.3 A representation on attendance relief has been submitted to the office of the Dean (Academics) and
a decision is awaited.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## 3. Grievance Camp

3.1 A grievance camp will be held at the student activity centre on 28 September 2026 between 1600 and
1800 hours for students who wish to record their attendance-related complaints.

3.2 Students are advised to bring their attendance printouts from the student portal to the camp.
