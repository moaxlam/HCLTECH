# Fee Notice FEE-NOTICE-2026-07: Semester Fee, Examination Fee and Late Fee

Doc ID: FEE-NOTICE-2026-07
Version: 1.0
Issued by: Office of the Finance Officer
Effective from: 2026-07-01

<!-- page: 1 -->
## 1. Semester Fee for the Odd Semester 2026

1.1 The semester fee for the odd semester 2026 shall be paid on or before **10 August 2026**.

1.2 The fee payable is as follows.

| Programme | Tuition fee (INR) | Examination fee (INR) | Total (INR) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B.Tech CSE | 87500 | 2500 | 90000 |
| B.Tech ECE | 82500 | 2500 | 85000 |

1.3 The examination fee of INR 2500 is payable whether or not the student appears for a supplementary
examination in that semester.

## 2. Late Fee

2.1 A student who pays the semester fee after 10 August 2026 but on or before 31 August 2026 shall pay
a late fee of **INR 500**.

2.2 A student who pays after 31 August 2026 shall pay a late fee of **INR 2000**.

2.3 A late fee once paid is not refundable.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## 3. Supplementary Examination Fee

3.1 The fee for a supplementary examination is INR 500 per course, as notified in the Examination
Rules and Procedures.

3.2 The fee for rechecking is INR 200 per course and for revaluation INR 500 per course.

## 4. Concessions

4.1 A student who is a recipient of the Merit Scholarship under circular SCHOLARSHIP-2026-01 receives a
fee concession of 25 percent of the semester fee.

4.2 Fee concessions under any State or Central government scheme are applied on production of the
sanction order issued by the competent authority. A student who does not produce the sanction order by
the notified date must pay the fee and seek a refund after production of the order.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## 5. Non-Payment

5.1 A student who has not paid the semester fee by 30 September 2026 shall not be permitted to register
for the next semester and the hall ticket for the end-semester examination may be withheld.

5.2 The Finance Office shall not accept payment by cash above INR 10000. Payments are accepted through
the University payment gateway or by demand draft.

5.3 A student who believes that a fee demand is incorrect shall represent to the Finance Officer within
fifteen days of the demand with supporting documents.
