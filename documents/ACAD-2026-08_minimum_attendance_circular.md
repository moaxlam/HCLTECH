# Circular ACAD-2026-08: Revised Minimum Attendance for End-Semester Examinations

Doc ID: ACAD-2026-08
Version: 1.0
Issued by: Office of the Registrar
Effective from: 2026-08-01

<!-- page: 1 -->
## 1. Revised minimum attendance

1.1 With effect from 01 August 2026, the minimum attendance required in each registered course for a
student to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination is **80 percent** of the classes
held in that course.

1.2 This circular **supersedes clause 7.2 of the Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Programmes
(ACAD-REG-2024)** and any other provision fixing the minimum attendance for end-semester
examinations at any other value.

1.3 The provision of clause 7.3 of ACAD-REG-2024 relating to condonation by the Dean (Academics) shall
continue to apply, provided that the attendance after condonation is not less than 70 percent in the
course concerned.

1.4 The provision of clause 7.4 of ACAD-REG-2024 relating to detention shall continue to apply.

## 2. Applicability

2.1 This circular applies to all undergraduate programmes and to students of all batches admitted
from 2023 onwards.

2.2 This circular applies to end-semester examinations conducted from the 2026-DEC session onwards.
Attendance continuing from an earlier session is computed under the value in force on the date of
computation.

## 3. Rationale

3.1 The revision follows a review of student performance in the 2026-MAY session and is intended to
strengthen classroom engagement in laboratory-linked and tutorial-linked courses.

3.2 Departments shall display this circular on their notice boards and communicate it to all
course coordinators before the start of the next session.

## 4. Clarifications

4.1 A shortage of attendance, however small, is a shortage. The minimum is not a target and no
rounding of the attendance percentage shall be applied in the student's favour.

4.2 Requests for condonation continue to be decided by the Dean (Academics) on the merits of each
case, and this circular does not create any entitlement to condonation.
