# Examination Rules and Procedures

Doc ID: EXAM-RULES-2025
Version: 2.0
Issued by: Office of the Controller of Examinations
Effective from: 2025-01-01

<!-- page: 1 -->
## 1. Scope

1.1 These rules describe how end-semester and supplementary examinations are conducted. They are read
together with the Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Programmes (ACAD-REG-2024).

1.2 Where these rules and the Academic Regulations differ on a matter of eligibility, the Academic
Regulations, as amended by any circular in force, prevail.

## 2. Examination Sessions

2.1 The University conducts two end-semester examination sessions each year: 2026-MAY for even
semester courses and 2026-DEC for odd semester courses.

2.2 The supplementary examination is conducted once for each session, ordinarily within eight weeks of
the declaration of the result of that session.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## 3. Marks and Pass Requirement

3.1 A theory course is examined out of 100 marks: internal assessment of 30 marks and an
end-semester paper of 70 marks.

3.2 The minimum pass mark in a course is **40 marks out of 100** in the aggregate.

3.3 In addition, a student must secure at least 30 percent of the maximum marks of the end-semester
paper. A student who secures the aggregate minimum but less than 30 percent of the paper marks is
declared FAIL in that course.

3.4 Marks are not rounded up. A total of 39.5 marks is a FAIL.

## 4. Registration for the End-Semester Examination

4.1 A student must register for the end-semester examination of each registered course through the
student portal within the notified window.

4.2 An examination fee is payable for each registered paper as notified by the Finance Office.

4.3 The hall ticket is issued only to students who have registered and paid the examination fee.

## 5. Supplementary Examination Procedure

5.1 A student who wishes to appear for a supplementary examination shall apply through the student
portal **within 10 days** of the declaration of the result of the session concerned.

5.2 An application fee of INR 500 per course is payable. Applications received after the notified date
may be considered with the approval of the Controller of Examinations on payment of a late fee of
INR 200.

5.3 The office verifies the student's eligibility before the supplementary examination, including the
attendance requirement in force under the Academic Regulations as amended.

5.4 A student whose application is rejected may represent to the Controller of Examinations within
seven days.

<!-- page: 7 -->
## 6. Absence, Withdrawal and Unfair Means

6.1 A student who does not appear for a registered end-semester paper without approved leave is
recorded as ABSENT in that course.

6.2 ABSENT is treated as a failure for the purpose of clearing the course, but the student may apply
for the supplementary examination subject to eligibility.

6.3 Cases of unfair means are referred to the Examination Disciplinary Committee. A course involved in
a pending unfair-means case is withheld and is not reported as PASS or FAIL until the case is decided.

## 7. Revaluation and Rechecking

7.1 A student may apply for rechecking of the totalling of marks within 15 days of declaration of the
result on payment of the notified fee.

7.2 Revaluation of a paper is permitted for a maximum of two courses per session and must be applied
for within 15 days of declaration of the result.

<!-- page: 9 -->
## 8. Declaration of Results

8.1 Results are declared on the University portal. The University does not communicate individual
results over the telephone.

8.2 A result once declared is not amended except by revaluation, rechecking or a decision of the
Examination Disciplinary Committee.
