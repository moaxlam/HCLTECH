# Hostel Handbook 2026

Doc ID: HOSTEL-RULES-2026
Version: 4.0
Issued by: Office of the Chief Warden
Effective from: 2026-07-01

## 1. Admission to Hostel

1.1 Hostel seats are allotted for one academic year at a time. Allotment priority is given in the
following order: students admitted from outside the State, students of the final year, students
receiving a scholarship, and remaining applicants by distance from the University.

1.2 A student allotted a seat must occupy it within seven days of allotment, failing which the
allotment is treated as cancelled and the seat is offered to the next applicant.

1.3 A student who wishes to vacate the hostel in the middle of the year must apply to the Chief Warden
at least fifteen days in advance.

## 2. Hostel Fee

2.1 The hostel fee for the academic year 2026-27 is INR 42000, which includes the mess advance of
INR 12000. The fee must be paid within the date notified with the allotment.

2.2 The mess advance is adjusted against the mess charges for the semester and any balance is refunded
on vacating the hostel.

2.3 A late fee of INR 500 applies to hostel fee payments received after the notified date.

## 3. Discipline and Timings

3.1 All residents shall return to the hostel by 2130 hours. A resident who needs to return later shall
apply for out-pass in advance through the warden of the block.

3.2 Night out-pass is granted only for a valid reason and with the approval of the warden of the block,
and for a maximum of three nights in a month.

3.3 Smoking, consumption of alcohol, and possession of any intoxicant in the hostel premises are
strictly prohibited and attract disciplinary action including expulsion from the hostel.

3.4 Loud music and any activity that disturbs other residents between 2200 hours and 0700 hours is not
permitted.

## 4. Leave and Attendance of Residents

4.1 A resident proceeding on leave shall inform the warden of the block and record the leave in the
hostel register before leaving.

4.2 A resident absent from the hostel for more than seven consecutive days without recorded leave may
lose the seat.

4.3 Illness must be reported to the warden of the block. The hostel medical room is available round the
clock for first aid only.

## 5. Maintenance and Damage

5.1 Furniture in a room is allotted against a signed inventory at the time of occupying the room.

5.2 Loss of or damage to hostel property shall be recovered from the residents of the room, unless the
resident responsible is identified, in which case it is recovered from that resident.

5.3 Any repair request shall be entered in the maintenance register of the block.

<!-- page: 7 -->
## 6. Vacation, Vacating and Refund

6.1 Residents shall vacate the hostel during the notified vacation periods unless permitted to stay by
the Chief Warden for a valid academic reason.

6.2 A resident who vacates the hostel during the academic year is refunded the hostel fee for the
remaining complete months on a pro-rata basis, less the mess charges already incurred.

6.3 The room key and the inventory must be surrendered at the time of vacating. A charge of INR 200 is
recovered for a lost key.
