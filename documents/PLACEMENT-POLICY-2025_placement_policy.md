# Placement Policy

Doc ID: PLACEMENT-POLICY-2025
Version: 1.2
Issued by: Training and Placement Cell
Effective from: 2025-07-01

## 1. Scope and Applicability

1.1 This policy governs eligibility of students to participate in campus placement drives conducted by
the Training and Placement Cell.

1.2 This policy applies to all undergraduate programmes and to batches admitted from 2023 onwards.

1.3 This policy does not by itself relax any academic requirement of the University. A student who is
ineligible under the Academic Regulations remains ineligible for a drive, irrespective of this policy.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## 2. Eligibility for Placement Drives

2.1 A student is eligible to register for a campus placement drive if the student:

a. has a CGPA of **6.00 or more**; and
b. has **not more than one active backlog**; and
c. is not detained in any course under clause 7.4 of ACAD-REG-2024; and
d. has no pending unfair-means case.

2.2 Eligibility is assessed on the declared results available on the date of the drive. Results declared
after the eligibility list is published are not applied to that drive.

2.3 A student who is ABSENT or FAIL in a course retains an active backlog until the course is cleared.

2.4 The Training and Placement Cell may, for a particular drive, apply the eligibility criteria notified
by the recruiting organisation where those criteria are stricter than clause 2.1. Criteria notified by a
recruiter do not apply to other drives.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## 3. Registration and Participation

3.1 A student must register for each drive separately within the notified window.

3.2 A student who is eligible and selected by a recruiting organisation on the placement of record must
withdraw from the further drives as notified.

3.3 Misrepresentation of CGPA, backlogs or attendance in a placement application is a disciplinary
matter.

## 4. Internships

4.1 Internship eligibility is assessed under clause 2.1 unless an exception is notified for a specific
internship programme.

4.2 A student with an active backlog may be permitted to take up an internship only with the written
approval of the Head of Department and the Training and Placement Officer.

<!-- page: 6 -->
## 5. Grievances

5.1 A student who believes that eligibility has been computed incorrectly may represent to the
Training and Placement Officer within seven days of the publication of the eligibility list, with the
relevant course-wise results and attendance.

5.2 The Training and Placement Officer shall decide the representation after consulting the office of
the Controller of Examinations where necessary.
