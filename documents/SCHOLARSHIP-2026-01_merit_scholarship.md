# Circular SCHOLARSHIP-2026-01: Merit Scholarship for the Academic Year 2026-27

Doc ID: SCHOLARSHIP-2026-01
Version: 1.0
Issued by: Office of the Dean (Student Welfare)
Effective from: 2026-07-15

<!-- page: 1 -->
## 1. Scholarship

1.1 The University invites applications for the Merit Scholarship for the academic year 2026-27 from
students of all undergraduate programmes admitted from 2023 onwards.

1.2 The scholarship carries a fee concession of 25 percent of the semester fee for two semesters.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 An applicant must satisfy all of the following as on the last date of application:

| Requirement | Minimum value |
| --- | --- |
| CGPA | 7.50 |
| Attendance in every registered course | 80 percent |
| Active backlogs | 0 |
| Disciplinary action | None |

2.2 The attendance requirement in clause 2.1 is the requirement in force for the session concerned. A
student granted condonation under the Academic Regulations is treated as satisfying the attendance
requirement only to the extent of the condonation granted.

2.3 A student who has previously received the Merit Scholarship twice is not eligible.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## 3. Application

3.1 Applications shall be submitted on the student portal between 01 September 2026 and 30 September
2026. No application shall be accepted after 30 September 2026.

3.2 The application must include the student's latest result statement and a declaration of attendance
for each registered course. The office verifies attendance and results against University records and
does not rely on the student's declaration.

3.3 Incomplete applications are rejected without further notice.

## 4. Selection and Disbursement

4.1 Applications are ranked by CGPA and the scholarship is awarded to the top applicants within the
budget of the scheme.

4.2 The list of awardees is published on the portal. The fee concession is applied to the next semester
fee demand.

4.3 A scholarship once awarded may be withdrawn if the awardee's attendance in any course falls below
the requirement during the period of the scholarship.
