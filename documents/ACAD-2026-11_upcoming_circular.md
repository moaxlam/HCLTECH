# Circular ACAD-2026-11: Condonation and Detention, Revised

Doc ID: ACAD-2026-11
Version: 1.0
Issued by: Office of the Dean (Academics)
Effective from: 2026-12-01

<!-- page: 1 -->
## 1. Revised Condonation and Detention

1.1 With effect from **01 December 2026**, the condonation that the Dean (Academics) may grant under
clause 7.3 of the Academic Regulations is revised to a maximum of **15 percent** of the classes held,
provided that the attendance after condonation is not less than **70 percent** in the course concerned.

1.2 With effect from the same date, a student whose attendance in a course is below the minimum by not
more than three percentage points shall be placed in a supervised academic recovery programme instead
of being detained, provided the student clears the course assessments of that programme.

1.3 This circular supersedes clause 7.3 of ACAD-REG-2024 (as modified by circular ACAD-2026-08) with
effect from 01 December 2026.

## 2. Transitional Provision

2.1 Attendance for a session that began before 01 December 2026 continues to be governed by the value
in force on the date of computation of the attendance.

2.2 The examination of a course in which a student is placed in the academic recovery programme under
clause 1.2 shall be conducted by the Controller of Examinations in consultation with the Dean
(Academics).

## 3. Note on this Circular

3.1 This circular is issued in advance for information and shall be acted upon only from its effective
date. It does not create any right before 01 December 2026.
