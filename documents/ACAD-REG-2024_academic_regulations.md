# Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Programmes

Version: 3.1
Issued by: Office of the Dean (Academics)
Effective from: 2024-07-01

## 1. Preamble and Scope

1.1 These regulations govern the admission, registration, attendance, examination and promotion of
students of all undergraduate programmes of the University.

1.2 These regulations apply to every student admitted to a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) programme
from the academic year 2023-24 onwards, unless a provision states otherwise.

1.3 Where a provision of these regulations conflicts with a circular or notification issued by an
authorised office of the University, the conflict shall be resolved under the University's Source
Precedence Policy.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Batch" means the calendar year of first admission to the programme, for example a student first
admitted in 2023 belongs to batch 2023.

2.2 "Course" means a single taught subject identified by a course code, for example CS201.

2.3 "Classes held" means the number of scheduled contact sessions of a course conducted up to the date
of computation.

2.4 "Active backlog" means a course in which the student has a result of FAIL, ABSENT or DETAINED and
which has not yet been cleared.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## 3. Programmes, Admission and Batches

3.1 The University offers, among others, B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (programme code
`B.Tech CSE`) and B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (programme code `B.Tech ECE`).

3.2 Every programme is divided into eight semesters of approximately sixteen weeks each.

## 4. Semester and Credit Structure

4.1 Course credits are assigned as follows.

| Course type | Credits | Contact sessions per week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theory | 3 | 3 |
| Theory with tutorial | 4 | 4 |
| Laboratory | 1.5 | 3 |
| Project | 2 | 2 |

4.2 A student must register for a minimum of 18 credits and a maximum of 26 credits in a semester.

## 5. Course Registration

5.1 Registration for a semester is complete only when the student has registered for courses and paid
the semester fee notified by the Finance Office.

5.2 A student who does not complete registration by the notified last date may register late with the
approval of the Dean (Academics).

<!-- page: 8 -->
## 6. Internal Assessment

6.1 Internal assessment in a theory course is out of 30 marks, made up of two mid-semester tests and
continuous assessment, as notified by the course coordinator.

6.2 Internal assessment in a laboratory course is out of 30 marks and includes the laboratory record
and viva performance.

6.3 A student who has not attended the minimum number of laboratory sessions required by the
department may be refused a laboratory internal assessment mark.

<!-- page: 14 -->
## 7. Attendance and Detention

7.1 Attendance shall be recorded for every scheduled session of every registered course. Attendance
percentage for a course is computed as classes attended divided by classes held, multiplied by 100,
rounded to one decimal place.

7.2 Minimum attendance for end-semester examination: A student shall have a minimum of **75 percent**
of the classes held in each registered course in order to be eligible to appear for the end-semester
examination of that course. This clause applies to all programmes and all batches.

7.3 Condonation: The Dean (Academics) may condone attendance shortage of up to **10 percent**, on
medical grounds supported by documentary evidence, provided that the attendance after condonation is
not less than **65 percent** in the course concerned.

7.4 Detention: A student whose attendance in a course falls below 65 percent and who is not granted
condonation under clause 7.3 shall be detained in that course. A detained student is not eligible to
appear for the end-semester examination of that course and must repeat the course in a later semester.

7.5 Attendance shortage is computed for each course separately. Shortage in one course does not by
itself affect the eligibility of another course.

## 8. End-Semester Examination

8.1 End-semester examinations are conducted in the sessions 2026-MAY (even semester) and 2026-DEC
(odd semester), as notified by the Controller of Examinations.

8.2 A theory course carries a maximum of 100 marks for the purpose of the result: internal assessment
of 30 marks under clause 6.1 and an end-semester examination paper of 70 marks.

8.3 Pass requirement: A student passes a course when the total marks (internal plus external) are
**40 or more out of 100** and the end-semester paper carries not less than 30 percent of its maximum
marks. A course not cleared under this clause is recorded as FAIL, unless the student was ABSENT for
the end-semester examination.

8.4 A student who does not appear for the end-semester examination of a registered course without
approved leave is recorded as ABSENT in that course.

## 9. Supplementary Examinations

9.1 A student who has failed or been recorded ABSENT in a course may appear for the supplementary
examination of that course, provided the attendance requirement of clause 7.2 is satisfied for that
course.

9.2 A student may appear for the supplementary examination of a course on not more than two
occasions. Eligibility is determined at the time of application by the office of the Controller of
Examinations using the attendance and result records of the student.

9.3 Marks obtained in the supplementary examination replace the end-semester paper marks, and the
result is recalculated under clause 8.3. The internal assessment marks are carried forward.

9.4 A detained student under clause 7.4 is not eligible for the supplementary examination of the
course in which the detention was imposed.

## 10. Results, Grading and CGPA

10.1 The result of a course is recorded as one of PASS, FAIL, ABSENT or DETAINED.

10.2 Letter grades are awarded as follows.

| Total marks | Grade | Grade points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90-100 | O | 10 |
| 80-89 | A+ | 9 |
| 70-79 | A | 8 |
| 60-69 | B+ | 7 |
| 50-59 | B | 6 |
| 40-49 | C | 5 |
| Below 40 | F | 0 |

10.3 CGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points of all courses completed by the student,
computed to two decimal places.

## 11. Backlogs

11.1 A course in which the result is FAIL, ABSENT or DETAINED remains an active backlog until the
student clears it under clause 8.3 or clause 9.3.

11.2 A student with active backlogs must register for the course again in a subsequent semester,
including for the supplementary examination where eligible.

## 12. Placement and Internship

12.1 Eligibility for campus placement drives is governed by the Placement Policy notified by the
Training and Placement Cell, read together with these regulations.

## 13. Fees

13.1 Semester fees, examination fees and late fees are as notified by the Finance Office from time to
time. Non-payment of the notified fee by the notified date may attract a late fee and may affect
registration under clause 5.1.

## 14. Grievance and Appeals

14.1 A student aggrieved by a decision taken under these regulations may represent in writing to the
Dean (Academics) within fifteen days of the communication of the decision.
